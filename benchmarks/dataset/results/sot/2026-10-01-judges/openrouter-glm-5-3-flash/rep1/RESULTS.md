### Q01 (A — End-to-end / cross-module synthesis)

> Trace the full lifecycle of a stock item from Sales Order through to final accounting: Sales Order, Delivery Note, and Sales Invoice. Given that a Delivery Note can be submitted well before the corresponding Sales Invoice, how does ERPNext ensure the cost of goods sold is booked exactly once rather than twice? Name the relevant functions, classes and files across all three doctypes.

**[0]** Sales Order itself posts no GL entries — it is not an accounting document; SalesOrder.on_submit() (sales_order.py) only handles credit limit, reserved qty, and approving-authority checks.

- ◐ **codegraph-jev** (partial): Candidate confirms Sales Order posts no GL entries but never mentions on_submit's credit-limit, reserved-qty, or approving-authority checks, instead describing a different reservation mechanism.
- ◐ **codegraph-laya** (partial): Confirms SO posts no GL entries but lists different functions (not credit limit/approving-authority checks) than the gold fact specifies.

**[1]** DeliveryNote.on_submit() (erpnext/stock/doctype/delivery_note/delivery_note.py) calls update_stock_ledger() (creating Stock Ledger Entries that reduce warehouse qty) and then make_gl_entries(), inherited from controllers/stock_controller.py, which books inventory (credit) against a provisional expense/COGS account for the delivered items.

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Candidate correctly identifies on_submit calling update_stock_ledger() and make_gl_entries() inherited from StockController in stock_controller.py, crediting inventory against the expense/COGS account.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Matches DN.on_submit calling update_stock_ledger to reduce warehouse qty, then make_gl_entries via stock_controller, and later details the provisional/COGS inventory credit booking.

**[2]** SalesInvoice.on_submit() (erpnext/accounts/doctype/sales_invoice/sales_invoice.py) calls update_billing_status_in_dn() and then make_gl_entries(), which goes through erpnext/accounts/doctype/sales_invoice/services/gl_composer.py (SalesInvoiceGLComposer).

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Candidate names update_billing_status_in_dn() and make_gl_entries() routing through SalesInvoiceGLComposer in gl_composer.py, matching the gold fact.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Explicitly names SalesInvoice.on_submit calling update_billing_status_in_dn then make_gl_entries routed through SalesInvoiceGLComposer in gl_composer.py.

**[3]** The reconciliation step that prevents double-booking COGS is the composer's stock-delivered-but-not-billed handling: for each invoice line referencing a delivery_note/dn_detail, it looks up the DN's Stock Ledger Entry value and the DN item's expense_account, and if that account is the provisional/SDBNB account, it reverses the provisional DN-side booking and re-books it as real COGS tied to the invoice.

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Candidate details the SDBNB reconciliation logic checking the DN's expense account and reversing/re-booking COGS, matching the gold fact's description.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Describes the reconciliation step looking up DN's SLE value and expense account, and reversing the provisional booking into real COGS tied to invoice via dn_detail.

**[4]** This means COGS is recognized exactly once even though the Delivery Note and Sales Invoice can be submitted at very different times — the invoice-side logic actively reverses what the delivery-note-side logic already posted, rather than both sides posting independently.

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Candidate explicitly states COGS is booked exactly once despite DN/SI timing differences, with invoice-side logic reclassifying what DN already posted.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): States COGS is booked once despite DN/SI timing gap, via the invoice-side reversal mechanism described throughout.

**[5]** If update_stock is set on the Sales Invoice itself (bypassing a separate Delivery Note), the invoice does its own update_stock_ledger() and stock-movement GL entries directly instead of going through the DN-reversal path.

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Candidate explains that when update_stock=1 on the Sales Invoice, it performs its own stock ledger update and GL entries instead of the DN-reversal path.

### Q21 (E — Concurrency & database semantics)

> When ERPNext runs on PostgreSQL, how does the stock reposting/valuation subsystem prevent concurrent background jobs from deadlocking or double-computing the average rate for the same item-warehouse batch valuation?

**[0]** repost_gate(item_code, warehouse) in erpnext/stock/stock_ledger.py returns a session-level Postgres advisory lock via frappe.db.advisory_lock(("stock_repost", item_code, warehouse), ...) when running on Postgres, or a no-op context manager otherwise (so MariaDB keeps relying on plain deadlock-retry).

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Answer states repost_gate returns advisory_lock keyed on stock_repost/item_code/warehouse on postgres, else nullcontext no-op.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Candidate correctly identifies repost_gate returning a session-level Postgres advisory_lock keyed on the stock_repost tuple, with nullcontext() fallback for non-Postgres.

**[1]** repost_gate is used as a context manager wrapping update_entries_after(...) inside repost_future_sle's loop, so row-level locks happen only after the advisory lock is acquired, avoiding lock-order deadlocks between concurrent reposts of the same item/warehouse.

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Answer confirms repost_gate wraps update_entries_after inside repost_future_sle's loop, matching gold fact.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Candidate states repost_future_sle wraps update_entries_after with the gate, matching the fact.

**[2]** The synchronous repost_current_voucher submit path is NOT wrapped by repost_gate, to avoid blocking a document submit behind a background repost.

- ❌ **codegraph-jev** (missing): Answer explicitly states repost_current_voucher is not wrapped by repost_gate and gives the rationale of not blocking submit.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Candidate explicitly states the submit path is not gated for this exact reason.

**[3]** BatchNoValuation.calculate_avg_rate in erpnext/stock/serial_batch_bundle.py takes a transaction-scoped advisory lock via frappe.db.transaction_advisory_lock keyed on ("batch-valuation", item_code, warehouse), only for outward transactions and only on Postgres.

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Answer matches the transaction-scoped advisory lock keyed on batch-valuation/item_code/warehouse for outward transactions on Postgres.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Candidate correctly names the file, function, lock call, key tuple, and conditions (outward/actual_qty<=0, Postgres-only).

**[4]** The rationale documented in code is that MariaDB serializes via grouped FOR UPDATE + gap locks on historical ledger reads, but Postgres has no gap locks, so a transaction-scoped advisory lock (auto-released at commit/rollback) serializes without either downside.

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Answer gives the rationale matching gold fact about MariaDB gap locks vs Postgres advisory lock released at commit/rollback.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Candidate reproduces the documented rationale almost exactly matching the gold fact.
