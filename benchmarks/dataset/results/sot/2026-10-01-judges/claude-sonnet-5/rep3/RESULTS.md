### Q01 (A — End-to-end / cross-module synthesis)

> Trace the full lifecycle of a stock item from Sales Order through to final accounting: Sales Order, Delivery Note, and Sales Invoice. Given that a Delivery Note can be submitted well before the corresponding Sales Invoice, how does ERPNext ensure the cost of goods sold is booked exactly once rather than twice? Name the relevant functions, classes and files across all three doctypes.

**[0]** Sales Order itself posts no GL entries — it is not an accounting document; SalesOrder.on_submit() (sales_order.py) only handles credit limit, reserved qty, and approving-authority checks.

- ◐ **codegraph-jev** (partial): Candidate correctly states SO posts no GL/stock entries but never names on_submit() or the specific credit-limit/reserved-qty/approving-authority checks it performs.
- ◐ **codegraph-laya** (partial): Candidate correctly says SO posts no GL entries and mentions reserved qty, but omits credit limit and approving-authority checks from on_submit().

**[1]** DeliveryNote.on_submit() (erpnext/stock/doctype/delivery_note/delivery_note.py) calls update_stock_ledger() (creating Stock Ledger Entries that reduce warehouse qty) and then make_gl_entries(), inherited from controllers/stock_controller.py, which books inventory (credit) against a provisional expense/COGS account for the delivered items.

- ◐ **codegraph-jev** (partial): Correctly describes update_stock_ledger()+make_gl_entries() and the credit-inventory/debit-expense behavior, but attributes the GL logic to DeliveryNoteGLComposer/BaseStockGLComposer rather than inheritance from controllers/stock_controller.py as the gold fact states.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Matches the gold fact's function, class/file, and behavior of reducing stock and booking GL against a provisional account.

**[2]** SalesInvoice.on_submit() (erpnext/accounts/doctype/sales_invoice/sales_invoice.py) calls update_billing_status_in_dn() and then make_gl_entries(), which goes through erpnext/accounts/doctype/sales_invoice/services/gl_composer.py (SalesInvoiceGLComposer).

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Candidate names update_billing_status_in_dn() and make_gl_entries() together with the SalesInvoiceGLComposer in services/gl_composer.py, matching the gold fact's required names.
- ◐ **codegraph-laya** (partial): Covers make_gl_entries() and the SalesInvoiceGLComposer/gl_composer.py file, but does not mention update_billing_status_in_dn() being called in on_submit().

**[3]** The reconciliation step that prevents double-booking COGS is the composer's stock-delivered-but-not-billed handling: for each invoice line referencing a delivery_note/dn_detail, it looks up the DN's Stock Ledger Entry value and the DN item's expense_account, and if that account is the provisional/SDBNB account, it reverses the provisional DN-side booking and re-books it as real COGS tied to the invoice.

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Candidate precisely describes the per-line DN SLE/expense_account lookup, the SDBNB account check, and the reclassification (reverse+rebook) logic matching the gold fact.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Describes the exact reconciliation mechanism: looking up the DN's SLE value via dn_detail and reversing/rebooking against the provisional account into real COGS.

**[4]** This means COGS is recognized exactly once even though the Delivery Note and Sales Invoice can be submitted at very different times — the invoice-side logic actively reverses what the delivery-note-side logic already posted, rather than both sides posting independently.

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Candidate explicitly states COGS is booked once net across DN+SI regardless of submission timing, matching the gold fact's point about single-booking via reversal.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Explicitly states COGS is booked exactly once via the invoice-side reversal mechanism regardless of timing gap between DN and SI.

**[5]** If update_stock is set on the Sales Invoice itself (bypassing a separate Delivery Note), the invoice does its own update_stock_ledger() and stock-movement GL entries directly instead of going through the DN-reversal path.

- ◐ **codegraph-jev** (partial): Candidate covers that update_stock on SI triggers its own stock-movement GL entries bypassing DN logic, but never mentions the invoice calling its own update_stock_ledger().

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Candidate correctly identifies repost_gate using a session-level Postgres advisory lock keyed on ("stock_repost", item_code, warehouse), with no-op fallback on MariaDB.

**[1]** repost_gate is used as a context manager wrapping update_entries_after(...) inside repost_future_sle's loop, so row-level locks happen only after the advisory lock is acquired, avoiding lock-order deadlocks between concurrent reposts of the same item/warehouse.

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Candidate correctly describes repost_gate as a context manager wrapping update_entries_after inside repost_future_sle's loop.
- ✅ **codegraph-laya** (covered): Exactly matches the gold fact that repost_gate wraps update_entries_after inside repost_future_sle's loop.

**[2]** The synchronous repost_current_voucher submit path is NOT wrapped by repost_gate, to avoid blocking a document submit behind a background repost.

- ✅ **codegraph-jev** (covered): Candidate explicitly states the synchronous submit path is not wrapped by repost_gate for this exact reason.
