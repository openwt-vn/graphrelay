## Statistical significance (McNemar's test)

Binary pass = coverage >= 75%.

**baseline vs graphrelay:** n=12, no discordant pairs (identical pass/fail)

With n=12 questions this test has real (if still modest) power. A 'not significant' result means the observed coverage gap could plausibly be noise, not that the arms are equivalent.
