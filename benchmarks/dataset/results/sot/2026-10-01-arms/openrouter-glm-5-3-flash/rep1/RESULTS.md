## Statistical significance (McNemar's test)

Binary pass = coverage >= 75%.

**baseline vs codegraph:** n=6, no discordant pairs (identical pass/fail)

**baseline vs graphify:** n=6, no discordant pairs (identical pass/fail)

**baseline vs codegraph-colby:** discordant pairs: 1 vs 0 (n=1) — scipy not installed, no p-value.

With n=6 questions this test has real (if still modest) power. A 'not significant' result means the observed coverage gap could plausibly be noise, not that the arms are equivalent.
